reaction-builder.html — open decisions
======================================

1. Covalent tabs: two drawn per edge, but only one real socket
---------------------------------------------------------------
Status:  deferred, working as described below
Code:    BOND_TABS / socketsOf()  (the "sockets" section)
         R_BOND and the tab drawing in pieceSVG()
         heightRows(), chainFits(), buildChainGroup()

The ask was a carbon block with two tabs on the left and two on the right,
with an oxygen plugging into each side.

What was built: carbon shows two bumps per edge and oxygen two holes, but
each EDGE IS ONE SOCKET. Sockets are matched by grid cell and an atom tile
is one row tall, so one cell per edge is all there is to match on. The two
bumps are the double bond in O=C=O drawn honestly; mechanically it is one
connection per side. O + C + O still snap into a single group and condense
into CO2, which is the behaviour that was actually wanted.

Why it was not done properly: two real sockets per edge needs two grid rows,
so carbon and oxygen atoms would become 2-row tiles. Those same atom species
are the pieces of every chain molecule, so:

  - H2O would render ragged: H (1 row), O (2 rows), H (1 row). Same for SO2,
    NO, NO2 — any chain mixing O with a 1-row atom.
  - chainFits() only checks row r for occupancy, never r+1, so a 2-row atom
    would silently overlap whatever sat below it. Real bug, would need fixing
    before tall atoms are safe.
  - buildChainGroup() places every atom of a chain on the same row and would
    need to centre them instead.
  - presets and layouts assume 1-row atoms; row budget is ROWS = 8.

If the two-socket geometry is wanted for real, that list is the work it
implies. Do not just change heightRows() — chainFits() is the trap.

2. Hydrogen has no bond tabs (related, also open)
--------------------------------------------------
Scope was deliberately limited to carbon and oxygen, so water can only be
made the ionic way: H -> H+ + e-, O + 2e- -> O2-, snap, condense. That works
and is tested, but it is a detour in combustion, where the chemistry is not
ionic at all.

Adding  H: "ctab"  to BOND_TABS would let H-O-H snap together directly,
since oxygen already carries the matching holes and H2O is already in the
CONDENSE table. One line, no other change needed — but it puts tabs on every
hydrogen tile on the board, including the ones in acids, which is the reason
it was left out.

Note both of these are cosmetic-vs-mechanical trade-offs, not correctness
bugs. Everything currently passes: node test.js (needs `npm install jsdom`).
