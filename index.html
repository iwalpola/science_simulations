Melting
Physics

Watch a snowman melt, and see where the energy comes from — mostly from the air, not the sunshine, because snow throws most of the light back. Then go inside the ice to watch the molecules break out of their pattern, while a heating curve tracks them and holds dead flat at 0 °C through the whole melt. Change the weather to change how long it takes.
