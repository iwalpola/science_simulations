Chemistry · molecular animation

The fourteen reactions, in three dimensions

One scene per reaction type. Each runs from the bench down to the individual ions and back, on a timeline you can scrub and pause. Every scene lets you swap the species — pick a different metal, a different acid — and the chemistry, colours, labels and equation follow. Built to sit alongside the Reaction Builder, which uses the same fourteen presets.
