Chemsheets help pages

Walk-through pages for the Chemsheets GCSE calculation worksheets. Each one explains the method, then works through that sheet's own questions — with the answers hidden until you press a button, so there is something left to think about.

GCSE 1411
Relative formula mass

How to read the periodic table — which of the two numbers is the mass — how to tell a balancing number from part of a formula, and how to lay out an Mr so you don't lose track. Includes a box where you can type any formula and see the working.

 GCSE 1413
Percentage by mass

The compound's mass drawn as a bar split into one slice per element, so you can see the answer before you calculate it. Covers brackets, hydrates, and writing a formula from a name.

 GCSE 1092
Moles

Mr first, then a ratio you cross-multiply. The same two rows answer "find the moles", "find the mass" and "find the Mr", so there is no formula to pick between. Includes unit conversions and significant figures.

 GCSE 1091
The Avogadro constant

A mole is a pack size. Every question is shown twice — once as shopping, once as the worksheet — with an animation of each, because the arithmetic is identical both times.

A sensible order. Relative formula mass comes first: percentage by mass and moles both begin by working one out, and neither will make sense without it.

Percentage by mass and moles can be done in either order. The Avogadro constant page explains why a mole is the size it is — useful alongside the moles page, but not needed to do the calculations.
