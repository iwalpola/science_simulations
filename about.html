I teach science, and I build the simulations I wish I had on the board when explaining an idea for the third time.

  • 6 yearsScience teacher at Colombo International School, Sri Lanka

My background is in mechanical engineering, and I have always been a maker and an electronics enthusiast who likes learning how to code better. That mix is where these pages come from: each one is a single HTML file you can open from a memory stick, shown on a projector, and built so the physics on screen is calculated rather than drawn to look about right.

Questions, corrections or ideas for a new simulation are welcome. The easiest way to reach me is through GitHub, or by opening an issue on the simulations repository.
