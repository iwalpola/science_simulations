Diamond cubic lattice

Every carbon is covalently bonded to four neighbours in a tetrahedral arrangement (sp³ hybridisation). All four valence electrons per atom are locked into σ-bonds.
Bonding (σ) electron pair — localized, tightly bound
Delocalized π electron — free to roam the sheet (metallic conduction in-plane)
