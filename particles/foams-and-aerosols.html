Foams and aerosols

What is trapped inside what, all the way down to the particles.

Two mixtures, turned inside out

Solid foamPockets of gas surrounded by a solid. Sponge, polystyrene, bread, pumice.
Liquid foamPockets of gas surrounded by a liquid. Shaving foam, whipped cream, bubble bath.
Solid aerosolTiny pieces of solid suspended in a gas. Smoke, diesel soot, dust.
Liquid aerosolTiny droplets of liquid suspended in a gas. Mist, fog, sprays, breath.

In a foam the gas is the part that is trapped. In an aerosol the gas is all around. Wherever they are, gas particles are far apart and move quickly and randomly; liquid particles touch and slide past each other; solid particles stay in a fixed pattern and only vibrate.
